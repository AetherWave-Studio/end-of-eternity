Bio

The end is near

"Synth echoes of the past from the future."Forged in the Colorado music scene

End of Eternity is a Denver trio that plays gothic synth-rock: romantic darkwave melody over driving drums, atmospheric synths and soaring guitar. The songs are about love, loss, hope and resilience, and the show is built to match them, with lasers, moving lights and a video wall that gives every song its own world.

Crow sings and plays synths. Drew Froehlich plays guitar and engineers the band's studio. John "JJ" Jouaneh is on drums. The three met through the Zen Lounge Podcast, which interviewed Crow's band AngelFlesh around 2014 and started a long musical correspondence with Drew and JJ of Zen Conspiracy. They formed End of Eternity in October 2023.

Their debut album Year Zero arrived in March 2024, followed by the singles "Shallow End" and "Haunted Dreams", all written and recorded in the band's own studio. In 2025 and 2026 they worked the Front Range club circuit, from Moe's in Englewood and the Trailside Saloon to Your Mom's House, The Rickhouse and Lost Lake in Denver, adding livestreamed sets along the way. The self-titled album is next, due April 20, 2027.

Live

Lights, lasers and a video wall. The band brings the show.

The band brings the production. Every song has its own visuals, and the room becomes part of the story.

  • Own lighting rig: moving heads, stage lasers and DMX control, run by the band.
  • Synced video wall. Song-by-song visuals run from a laptop at FOH over wireless HDMI.
  • Live drums, guitar, vocals and synths, with a consistent mix from room to room.
  • 45 to 55 minute headline set.

Stage plot and inputs

Lights: the band brings its own moving heads and lasers and runs them from the stage.

Video wall, very important: the video wall is a big part of the show. The band brings a wireless HDMI transmitter; the receiver plugs straight into the HDMI input at front of house, and the laptop broadcasts visuals synced to the performance. The only thing needed from the venue is an HDMI connection to the LED wall or projector.

Seven inputs, no backline needed. Drums are a digital kit on a stereo pair, guitar is a stereo pair, and vocals are one wireless mic.
